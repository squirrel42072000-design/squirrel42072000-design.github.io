Meet your Rootling

This little friend grows when you take small moments to care for yourself — a glass of water, a stretch, an honest check-in. No pressure, no guilt. Just gentle growing, together.

Welcome to Rootling

Rootling is a small pet that lives on this device. It helps you take small steps to care for yourself. A glass of water counts. A short rest counts. There is no score. Your pet is never sad if you miss a day.

Your information, in short

Kept on this device:

  • Your pet and its name
  • Your check-ins
  • Your settings (your choices for how the app looks and acts)
  • Things your pet learns, if you let it learn
  • Words you write in lists or journals in the app

Optional, off unless you turn it on:

  • Reminders (alerts from your pet)
  • Connected helpers, if any are added later — each one will ask first, in plain words, before it touches anything

You choose

These are the real switches. What you pick here is what happens.

Reminders: off. Rootling does not send reminders unless you turn them on later, in the app, in the Alerts card.

Analytics: Rootling collects none. There is no switch, because there is nothing to switch off. (Analytics means counting how people use an app. Rootling does not do that.)

You do not need to share private things

You do not need to share medical diagnoses, financial information, passwords, or anything private for Rootling to help you. Write only what you are comfortable keeping on this device.

A diagnosis is the name a doctor gives to a health problem. You never need to write one here.

                                                                        Privacy Policy, in plain words

                                                                        Last updated: October 15, 2026. This page is the whole policy. There is no longer version hidden somewhere else.

                                                                        What Rootling keeps

                                                                        Rootling keeps things on this device only. It keeps:

                                                                        • Your pet, its name, and how much it has grown.
                                                                        • Your account on this device: your name, your email, and your password kept scrambled — never as plain words.
                                                                        • Your companions collection: every companion you adopt, and which one is your main companion.
                                                                        • Your check-ins (your mood, water, movement, and sleep notes).
                                                                        • Your settings — the choices you make for how the app looks and acts.
                                                                        • Things your pet learns about how you use the app, but only if you turn learning on.
                                                                        • Anything you write in the app, such as lists or journal words (a journal is a place where you write your own notes).

                                                                        Some memories in your Memory Book are marked "Right now". After 30 days, Rootling points at them and asks if you want to keep them for later. Nothing is deleted unless you tap Forget.

                                                                        These things are kept in your browser's storage, on this device. A browser is the app you use to open web pages, like Chrome or Safari.

                                                                        Why Rootling keeps it

                                                                        So your pet can be your companion. That is the only reason. Your pet needs its name, your check-ins, and your choices so it can greet you and keep growing with you.

                                                                        What Rootling never does

                                                                        • Rootling never sells your information.
                                                                        • Rootling never uses your information for advertising (ads).
                                                                        • Rootling never shares your information with anyone, unless you say it may.
                                                                        • Rootling has no hidden tracking. Tracking means secretly watching what you do. Rootling does not do that.
                                                                        • Rootling never sends your account, or anything else, from this device to anyone. Your sign-in works on this device only.
                                                                        • As Rootling works today, the app sends nothing from this device to anyone. Your pet, check-ins, and learning stay here, on this device.

                                                                        Your choices

                                                                        • Learning on or off: in the adoption form, and later in Settings under "Learning". The first time you turn it on, Rootling asks once more, in plain words, so you can be sure.
                                                                        • Reminders on or off: in the Alerts card on the main screen. Reminders are off until you turn them on.
                                                                        • Download your data: tap "Download my data" at the top of this page, or "Download my pet's memory" in "What your companion has learned". You get one file that is yours to keep.
                                                                        • Delete some of your data: buttons at the top of this page can delete one part at a time, when that part exists.
                                                                        • Delete all of your data: tap "Delete my data" at the top of this page. It asks you to be sure first. Then it deletes everything Rootling keeps on this device.
                                                                        • See what your pet learned: tap "What your companion has learned" in Settings. You can forget what it learned there, too.

                                                                        How long things are kept

                                                                        Things are kept until you delete them. Rootling does not delete your pet or your check-ins on its own.

                                                                        One thing is not in Rootling's control: your browser can clear site data. Site data means the things a web page stores on your device. If you clear it, or if your device runs very low on space, your browser may remove what Rootling keeps. That is why the backup file matters: the backup file is the copy you control. Keep it somewhere safe if you want a copy that no browser can take away.
